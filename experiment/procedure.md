### Procedure

1. **Choose or Upload an Image**  
   - You will see a group of images displayed on the screen.  
   - Select one image from the options provided.  
   - Alternatively, if you want to see an image that you had locally, you can upload your own image to use in the experiment.

2. **View the Image with Normal Vision**  
   - Once an image is selected, it will first appear as it looks to someone with normal color vision.

3. **Explore Color Blindness Simulations**  
   - Below the image, you will find three buttons, each representing a different type of color blindness:  
     - **Protanopia**: Displays how the image appears to someone with red-color blindness.  
     - **Tritanopia**: Displays how the image appears to someone with blue-color blindness.  
     - **Color Blind**: Displays the image as shades of gray, simulating complete color blindness.  
   - Click any of the buttons to view the selected image through that specific color-blind perspective.

4. **Repeat or Try Another Image**  
   - Feel free to go back and select another image or upload a different one to explore more simulations.
